# Digital Drop material sources

The photographic textures and HDR environment in this directory are by Poly Haven,
licensed CC0 1.0: https://polyhaven.com/license . They may be redistributed with the game.

- Asphalt 04: https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_04
- Concrete Wall 007: https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_wall_007
- Leafy Grass: https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass
- Bark Brown 01: https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_01
- Denim Fabric: https://polyhaven.com/a/denim_fabric
- Kloppenheim 06 (Pure Sky): https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_06_puresky

`sources.json` records original download URLs and MD5 checksums. All files are the
original 1K downloads; no runtime connection to Poly Haven is required. Only the
normal and roughness channels of the denim scan are used, preserving Monova's
orange costume artwork.
